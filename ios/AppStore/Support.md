# Project Peon for iPhone and iPad — Support

Build a cart, test your design, and transport the peons to the ship across Earth, the Moon and Mars. Requires iOS or iPadOS 15 or later.

Drag components to build a cart. Use two fingers to rotate parts while editing. Tap the green Play button to drive. Choose touch or tilt controls in Settings. With touch controls, press the boost button to activate boosters; use **Position Boost Button** in Settings to drag it to a comfortable position. Reset restores its default position on the left.

The Save icon opens your saved cart creations. Use the cart-plus button to save a design; select a thumbnail and its green check mark to load it. The red trash button deletes a saved cart.

While building, the game menu's **pan camera** option lets you explore the map. Open the menu again and choose **return to building** to continue editing. Camera pan is unavailable while the cart load/save screen is open.

All motor and booster upgrades are included. No account or in-app purchase is required. The game works offline; saved carts, settings and progress stay on your device. Gameplay recording is unavailable on iPhone and iPad.

## Get help

[Report a problem or ask a question](https://github.com/david-a-campbell/ProjectPeon/issues). Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, level, and what happened. GitHub issues are public; avoid posting private information.
