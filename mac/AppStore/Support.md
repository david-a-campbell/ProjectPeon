# Project Peon for Mac — Support

Project Peon is a cart construction and physics driving game for Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 12 or later.

## Controls

Click and drag to build a cart. Click the green Play button to drive.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A / D or left / right arrows | Drive |
| Space | Boost |
| R | Relaunch |
| C | Return to building |
| P | Toggle map pan / follow cart |
| M | Next music track |
| Escape | Close an open menu |

The Save icon opens your saved cart creations. Use the cart-plus button to save a design; select a thumbnail and its green check mark to load it.

All motor and booster upgrades are included in the Mac game. No account or in-app purchase is required. Progress and saved carts stay on your Mac.

## Gameplay recording

Enable recording in the game's Settings before driving. The camera button on the results screen lets you choose where to save a silent MP4. Returning to building, restarting, changing levels, or quitting discards an unexported recording.

## Get help

[Report a problem or ask a question](https://github.com/david-a-campbell/ProjectPeon/issues). Include your macOS version, whether your Mac uses Apple silicon or Intel, the level, and what happened. Avoid posting private information in a public issue.
