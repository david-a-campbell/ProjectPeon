Level-select wide maps preserve every original center tile and all planet/moon placeholders. Each tile layer adds 32 reflected edge columns on both sides, and is positioned 512 points left to preserve its original coordinates. The extra coverage accommodates the 16:9 viewport and the existing mouse parallax range. Planet and moon sprites retain their original size and aspect ratio. Hex scenery repeats across both edges. These maps are Mac-only; iOS uses the original maps.
